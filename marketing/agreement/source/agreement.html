1. Service. The Company gives the Hostel access to HostelPalika — the web portal and mobile app for rooms, residents, rent and payments, food, attendance, notices and complaints — and a public listing page on hostelpalika.com. The features and limits of the chosen plan (residents, warden and cook logins) are those published on hostelpalika.com on the day of payment.

2. Plan Period. The plan starts on the day the Hostel goes live on HostelPalika and runs for the chosen billing period, counted in Bikram Sambat months. Each renewal starts a new period at the price published at the time of renewal. A price change never affects a period already paid for.

3. Fees and Payment. Each period is paid in advance. Each invoice is due by the end of the third (3rd) day after it is issued. The Owner shall pay only to the Company — through the payment link in the invoice email, or the Company’s official QR or bank account — and the Company issues an official receipt for every payment. The Company is not responsible for money handed to any person without an official Company receipt. Amounts are in Nepali Rupees; any tax required by law is shown on the invoice.

4. Reminders and Suspension. The Company sends reminders by email and app notification before and after a due date. If a due stays unpaid, the Company may send a written suspension notice; three (3) days after that notice the Hostel’s access to the portal and app is paused, except the billing page. Paying the balance in full restores access automatically. No data is deleted during a suspension.

5. Refunds. Plan fees are not refunded once a period has started, including for unused days. A payment made twice or charged in error is refunded in full, and if the Company ends this Agreement without fault of the Owner, the unused full months of a prepaid plan are refunded. Refunds go to the account the payment came from. A payment dispute must be raised within fourteen (14) days of the payment.

6. Residents’ Money. Rent, deposits and fees that residents pay the Hostel through HostelPalika (eSewa, Khalti, bank or QR) go straight to the Hostel’s own account. The Company does not collect, hold or refund this money. The Hostel sets its own fees and refund rules and is responsible for its own accounts and taxes.

7. Online Bookings. If the Hostel accepts online bookings, the guest pays the booking fee to the Company. The Hostel shall confirm or decline each booking within 24 hours and keep the bed free for the hold period. The part of the fee not refunded to the guest is shared (currently 60% Hostel, 40% Company) and paid to the Hostel’s verified payout account. The fee, hold period, share and refund steps are those published on hostelpalika.com on the day of booking. Repeated missed or cancelled bookings pause bookings for the Hostel.

8. Owner’s Responsibilities. The Owner shall hold every registration and licence the law requires to run the Hostel; keep the listing true and current (real photos, prices and vacancies); not refuse residents for caste, religion or ethnicity; keep logins private and answer for every action taken from the Hostel’s accounts, including wardens’ and cooks’; and not use the platform unlawfully or for spam.

9. Resident Data. The Hostel shall collect residents’ and guardians’ details, ID documents and photos with their consent, as required by the Individual Privacy Act, 2075. The Company uses this data only to provide the service, keeps ID documents in private storage, never sells it, and shares it only with the service providers needed to run the platform or when the law requires.

10. Ownership. The Hostel’s data belongs to the Hostel. On written request made before, or within thirty (30) days after, closing the account, the Company provides a copy of the Hostel’s resident and payment records; after that it may delete the data, except records the law requires it to keep. The HostelPalika software, name and brand belong to the Company.

11. Service and Support. The Company shall run the platform with reasonable care, fix faults promptly and give support by phone and email. Features may change, but no main feature of a plan is removed during a paid period without notice. The Company is not responsible for failures of internet, power, mobile networks, app stores or payment services.

12. Liability. The Company is not liable for disputes between the Hostel and its residents, guardians, staff or service providers, or for loss from wrong information the Hostel enters. Its total liability is limited to the plan fees paid in the twelve (12) months before a claim.

13. Termination. The Owner may end this Agreement by not renewing, or by written notice to work@softmato.com. The Company may suspend or end it by written notice for non-payment, false information, unlawful activity or serious breach. Amounts due remain payable.

14. Other Terms. Each party shall keep the other’s non-public information confidential. The HostelPalika Terms and Privacy Policy on hostelpalika.com also apply; where they differ, this Agreement prevails. Notices sent to the email addresses in this Agreement are valid. Changes to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties.

15. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Nepal. Disputes shall first be settled mutually, failing which they shall be resolved under the jurisdiction of Kathmandu.

16. Declaration. The Owner confirms being above 18 years of age and the owner or authorised operator of the Hostel, that the details given are true, and having read and understood this Agreement and signing it voluntarily.
